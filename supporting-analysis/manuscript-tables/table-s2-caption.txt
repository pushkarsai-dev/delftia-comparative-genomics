Table S2. Formal exclusion log for the genome panel. The sixteen assemblies that entered panel construction and were then excluded, with the criterion each failed and the values on which the decision was made. This is the formal exclusion log, not a list of every assembly returned by the initial retrieval; assemblies that were never carried into panel construction are not recorded here. Twelve were excluded on redundancy or assembly quality alone; four on grounds bearing on species assignment or genome integrity. Retention thresholds were total length 6.0–7.3 Mb, contig count ≤200, contig N50 ≥50 kb, G+C 65.5–68.0%, and CheckM completeness ≥95% with contamination ≤5%. ANI values against the study BAA-554 assembly are reported from the saved selection worksheet. For GCA_903684635.1, the 90.22% comparison with NBRC 16741 (GCF_001571325.1) and the 100% comparison with GCF_903815225.1 are FastANI results in the retained GTDB-Tk v2.3.2 output against GTDB R214. Assembly statistics are as recorded in GenBank on 27 July 2026. Species names are NCBI labels at retrieval.
