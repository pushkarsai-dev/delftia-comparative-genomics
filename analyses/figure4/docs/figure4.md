# Figure 4 caption

**Fig. 4. Organization and conservation of the delftibactin-associated region.**
(a) The 85.4 kb BAA-554 region at chromosome positions 2,159,932–2,245,340
(1-based, inclusive). Arrows indicate CDS positions and strands. Navy denotes
proteins detected in all 40 genomes, and orange denotes the four variable
proteins. Gray ticks identify the 20 proteins matching the top-ranked
KnownClusterBlast reference, delftibactin A/B cluster BGC0000984 (cumulative
BLAST score 38,447). Red outlines and labels identify HFJBPOEG_02821 and
HFJBPOEG_02843. (b) Gene order in 20 assemblies with at least 43 of the 45
reference proteins recovered by reciprocal searches within one selected
antiSMASH region. Colors indicate reference gene order; all tracks share a
physical scale and are aligned at their leftmost displayed gene. Red arrowheads
mark the three tracks reversed for display. Counts and spans are shown at
right. Gene order and relative strands were conserved after orientation
normalization. (c) Whole-proteome reciprocal-hit conservation: 41 of 45 proteins
were detected in all 40 genomes, and four in 25, 33, 37, and 39. Red triangles
mark the two highlighted proteins. Panels (b) and (c) use distinct search scopes;
genomic similarity does not establish metabolite production.

## Alt text

Gene arrows compare a delftibactin-associated region across 20 Delftia assemblies. Gene order is conserved after orienting three tracks. A bar chart shows 41 of 45 proteins detected in all 40 panel genomes.
