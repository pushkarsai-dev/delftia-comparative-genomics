# Figure 5 caption

**Fig. 5.** Domain assignments for two proteins within the delftibactin-associated region. (a) HFJBPOEG_02821, a 289 aa protein, contains PANTHER and Pfam LOG-family signatures corresponding to InterPro entries IPR052341 and IPR031100. Gene3D (G3DSA:3.40.50.450) and SUPERFAMILY (SSF102405, MCP/YpsA-like) provide broader structural assignments, rather than LOG-specific functional assignments. The conserved motif PGGFGTLDE spans residues 211-219. InterProScan detected no signature of a characterized pyridoxal-phosphate-dependent decarboxylase family in this protein. (b) HFJBPOEG_02843, a 329 aa protein, contains overlapping signatures consistent with a Fe(II)/2-oxoglutarate dioxygenase, including the Pfam TauD/TfdA domain PF02668. Bars show matched residue ranges, with one-based inclusive coordinates and archived E-values at right. The SSF102405 match comprises two fragments (41-84 and 115-282), joined by a thin line; the intervening gap does not indicate a deletion in the protein. Gray bars indicate full-length proteins on a common residue scale. Domain assignments are sequence-based predictions, not biochemical validation.
