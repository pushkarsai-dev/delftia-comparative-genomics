# Figure 3 caption

**Fig. 3. Protein-family detections across the 40 Delftia genomes.** Rows represent genomes, and columns show 11 of the 17 Pfam families searched (Table S1). Cell numbers give the count of predicted proteins matching each family at E < 1 × 10⁻³ in separate searches of each proteome. Zero denotes no detected match, not missing data. Neither PF00282 nor PF18631 was detected. The three displayed tryptophan-metabolism families had invariant counts across the panel. GCA_055379645.1 (red label, †) was the only genome with a UbiD-family hit and had an additional PF02441 hit assigned to candidate UbiX; the PF02441 hit shared by all genomes was assigned to a CoaC/DFP-like protein. These assignments do not establish an alternative route to tryptamine. Asterisks and bold black labels identify the four assemblies generated or reassembled in this study. Colors group counts as indicated; exact values are printed in every cell. Family detection does not establish substrate specificity or biochemical activity. PLP, pyridoxal 5′-phosphate; DC, decarboxylase; aa, amino acid; DAP, diaminopimelate; Orn, ornithine; Trp, tryptophan; IGPS, indole-3-glycerol phosphate synthase.

## Alt text

Heatmap of 40 Delftia genomes and 11 protein families. PF00282 and PF18631 have no hits. Three tryptophan-metabolism families have constant counts. One genome has UbiD and an extra UbiX/CoaC-like hit.
