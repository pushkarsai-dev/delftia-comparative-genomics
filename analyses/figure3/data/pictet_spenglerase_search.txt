Pictet-Spenglerase search — Delftia 40-genome panel
Date: 19 August 2026
Panel: 40 genomes, 239,999 proteins (pyrodigal 3.7.1, uniform re-annotation)

REFERENCE ENZYMES (retrieved from UniProtKB 19 Aug 2026)
  R4QPW9  McbB, Marinactinospora thermotolerans, 314 aa
          annotated "Cucumopine synthase"; the beta-carboline synthase of the
          marinacarboline pathway (Chen et al. 2013). Condenses L-tryptophan
          with an aldehyde.
  E4NJY1  Kitasatospora setae KSE_75260, 313 aa, Cucumopine_C domain protein
  E4NIM4  Kitasatospora setae KSE_70640, 317 aa, Cucumopine_C domain protein
          KslB, of the kitasetaline pathway, is one of these two.

  PF18631 against the three references:
     KSE_75260   E = 8.1e-55   score 172.6
     McbB        E = 2.7e-52   score 164.5
     KSE_70640   E = 8.4e-23   score  68.9

CONCLUSION
  No Pictet-Spenglerase of the characterised bacterial (McbB/KslB) type is
  present in any of the 40 Delftia genomes, by either method, while both
  methods detect the reference enzymes at high significance.

  Combined with the PF00282 result (0 hits in 239,999 proteins), Delftia
  encodes neither the decarboxylase that would generate tryptamine nor the
  condensing enzyme that would form the beta-carboline ring.

CAVEAT
  This excludes homologues of the two characterised bacterial enzyme types.
  It cannot exclude an unrelated enzyme performing the same chemistry, and
  the manuscript must say so.
