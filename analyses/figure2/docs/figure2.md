# Figure 2 caption and accessibility text

## Caption

**Fig. 2. Pangenome structure across the 40-genome panel.** (a) Total and core family counts across 36 phylogenetic clades. Open circles show individual clades; filled circles and connecting lines show means at each clade size. These clades are not independent random samples. The 39-to-40-genome comparison adds 312 families and reduces the core by three, yielding 21,853 total and 4,263 core families. (b) Family counts by genome occupancy, shown on a logarithmic vertical axis. Colors indicate core (4,263 families; 40 genomes), soft core (307; 38–39), shell (2,576; 6–37), and cloud (14,707; 1–5) categories.

## Alt text

With increasing clade size, total families generally increase and shared families decrease, reaching 21,853 and 4,263 at 40 genomes. A logarithmic histogram shows 11,544 families in one genome and 4,263 in all 40.

## Interpretation

Panel (a) compares the union and intersection of family membership within each
observed phylogenetic clade. It is not an ordered sequence of independent random
genome additions. Panel (b) counts how many families occur in exactly each number
of genomes; paralog copies do not increase genome occupancy. The final increment
is specific to the observed 39-to-40-genome comparison, not a universal per-genome
gain. No literature citation is embedded in this caption or figure.
